Privacy Policy for Clarity Reader

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Data Collection & Privacy

Clarity Reader operates 100% locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, reading texts, uploaded files, or optical prescription settings on any external servers.

2. Local Device Storage

Your visual preferences (such as diopter power, edge sharpness, contrast theme, and text size) are saved locally on your device using standard browser LocalStorage solely to restore your settings when you reopen the app.

3. Permissions

When you open a local text document, the file is processed entirely in-memory on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.

4. Contact

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store page.
